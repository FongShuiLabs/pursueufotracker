DROP 05 · 2026-08-07

Drop 05 - Bahia 1963, the FBI Triangles, and the Gulf of Oman

PURSUE Release 05 added 41 files to the archive - 22 documents, 16 videos, and 3 images - bringing the total to 375. It introduced a new releasing body: the Executive Office of the President, whose single file (EOP-UAP-D001) is a National Aeronautics and Space Council inquiry into a November 13, 1963 incident at Bahia, Brazil. Two State Department diplomatic cables from the U.S. Embassy in Rio de Janeiro, dated November 14 and 20, 1963, cover the same Bahia incident. The FBI contributed 17 records: eight FD-302 interview reports, eight digital renderings that the FBI prepared in 2026 as visual aids based on witnesses' first-hand descriptions (four of them depict triangular craft reported in 2002, 2011, and 2023), and one hand-held thermal-imaging video from a 2026 incident in the western United States. The Department of War added 19 files: historical intelligence records - a 1947 review of the Scandinavian "ghost rocket" incidents, a 1947-48 Air Materiel Command report, and a 1953 film analysis - plus unresolved UAP reports with sensor video from the Gulf of Oman (2021), the Pacific Ocean (2019), and the Middle East (2023-2025). The CIA added two records, a 1965 UFO report near Puerto Rico and undated briefing notes. Fourteen of the 41 files are partially redacted.
